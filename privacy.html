Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version. Neologos has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives your chats, keys or personal data. Your chats stay on your phone. When you use a feature that needs the internet, your phone talks directly to the service involved, such as the AI model provider you picked.

Who we are

Neologos is an iOS app made by Egehan Bakıcı, an individual developer in Türkiye ("we"), who is responsible for this app. Questions about this policy or your data: info@neologos.app.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run a server for Neologos and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We can't see what you do in the app, and we don't sell or share data, because we never have it.

Crash and speed reports. When the app crashes or freezes, and once a day about how fast it ran, iOS gives Neologos a technical report. These stay on your phone. They reach us only if you choose to send them by email from Settings › Help & about › Crash reports; they hold code addresses and timings, not your chats or keys.

What stays on your phone

  • Your chats, memories, notes, scheduled tasks, Home widgets and settings, protected by iOS file encryption. They're included in your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or a computer); you can leave chats out in Settings › Storage.
  • Your API keys and mail sign-in tokens, in the iOS Keychain, readable only on this device.
  • Website sign-ins made in the app's browser, kept per site and separate from Safari, plus the contact details you choose for filling forms.
  • Files and images the app makes for you, such as Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents.

You can delete all of it in Settings › Storage › Delete everything, or by deleting the app.

What leaves your phone, and only when you use it

AI model provider you pickYour message, the recent conversation and any results a tool found for it (for example an event, a contact, an email or a web page), sent with your own key to the provider of the model you chose: OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, or a server you added. Their privacy policy applies to what they receive. If you pick Apple's on-device model, nothing leaves your phone.
ImagesWhen you ask for an image, its description, and any photo or image you ask it to change, sent with your own key to OpenRouter or OpenAI.
WebsitesPages the app's browser opens for you, and what you approve it to type there.
Web search and page readingSearch queries and page addresses, sent to TinyFish if you added a TinyFish key, otherwise to your OpenRouter model's web search.
Gmail and OutlookIf you connect a mail account, mail moves directly between your phone and Google or Microsoft; nothing passes through us. When a request of yours needs mail, only the messages it needs go to the AI provider you picked, to answer it. See Google user data.
Weather and air qualityApproximate coordinates, sent to Open-Meteo.
Maps and placesPlace searches, handled by Apple Maps.
Stock and currency pricesThe symbols you ask about, sent to Yahoo Finance and Frankfurter.
Encyclopedia lookupsThe topic you ask about, sent to Wikipedia.
Servers you connect (MCP)Only the tool calls the app makes to that server.
DictationHandled by Apple's speech recognition.
App settings fileOnce a day the app downloads a small public file from this website that can pause a feature if a problem is found. Nothing about you is sent with it; like any web page, the host (GitHub) sees the request's IP address.

Google user data

If you connect Gmail, Neologos uses the Gmail permission (gmail.modify) from your phone only: it reads the messages a request of yours needs, saves drafts you ask for, sends a draft only when you tap Send, and archives or marks messages as read when you ask. It never deletes mail. Sign-in tokens stay in the iOS Keychain. To answer, the messages a request needs go to the AI provider you chose, under your own key. Remove the account in Settings › Mail to delete its tokens, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Neologos's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is never sold, never used for advertising, never read by people, and never used to train AI models.

Permissions

Neologos asks for Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Location, Photos, Camera, Microphone, Health, Home and Music access only when a feature needs it, and you can change each one in iOS Settings or in the app's Settings › Permissions. What a permission lets the app read is used only to answer the request you made.

Health data

Neologos reads activity, sleep, heart and workout data only when you ask about them, and never writes to Health. When you ask a health question, the relevant figures go to the model provider you picked so it can answer. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never sent anywhere else.

Actions on your behalf

Neologos asks before it pays, books, sends or deletes anything. Payments and bookings on websites need your explicit OK, confirmed with Face ID or your passcode when your phone has one, and bank and government sites always ask you to sign in yourself.

Purchases

You buy Neologos through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your name, Apple Account, card or address. Apple gives us only totals, such as how many copies sold in which country. AI usage is billed by the provider whose key you add, between you and them.

Your rights

Wherever you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK and California's CCPA, you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Because Neologos sends us nothing, the only data we may have is what you send us yourself, such as a support email or a crash report you chose to send. Write to info@neologos.app and we'll answer within 30 days. Everything in the app can be exported or deleted from Settings › Storage. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

When you email us, we keep the message only as long as we need it to help you, and at most two years.

Children

Neologos is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly receive data from them; we don't receive data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. If Neologos ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before the app does.
