Support

Neologos is an AI agent for your phone that browses, books, makes files and runs tasks on a schedule, and asks before it pays, books or sends. Get it on the App Store.

Need help or found a bug? Email info@neologos.app. Tell us what you asked, what happened and your iOS version. Please don't send API keys or passwords.

Getting started

  1. Open Neologos and tap Continue with OpenRouter to sign in, or add a key in Settings › Providers.
  2. Pick a model from the name at the top of the screen.
  3. Ask in plain words: "What's on my calendar tomorrow?", "Book the 10:00 ferry on Friday", "Every Monday, check this price for me".

Questions

What does it cost?

Neologos is a one-time purchase, with no subscription or in-app purchases. AI models are paid to the provider whose key you add (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others), usually a fraction of a cent per message. Some models are free.

Which key should I use?

OpenRouter is the easiest start: one key for hundreds of models. If you already pay OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral or xAI, add that key in Settings › Providers; when a model is on two keys, the direct one is used.

Will it buy or send things without me?

No. It stops at the final button on a website and asks you, with Face ID or your passcode. Mail is drafted for you to read and send. Scheduled tasks only report back; they never pay, book or send.

Where is my data?

On your phone. There's no Neologos account or server. Messages go only to the model provider you picked. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Storage › Delete everything removes your chats, keys, servers and settings from this phone. Deleting the app does the same.

How do I remove Neologos's access to my Gmail?

In Settings › Mail, remove the account: its sign-in tokens are deleted from your phone at once. You can also revoke access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Chats that mention mail are deleted with Settings › Storage › Delete everything, or one by one from your history. Neologos has no server, so nothing is left anywhere else.

Gmail says "access blocked"

Gmail sign-in is in testing while Google reviews it, so only invited testers can connect for now.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Charges from your AI provider are between you and that provider.

A scheduled task ran late

iOS decides the exact minute background tasks run. If your phone was off or in Low Power Mode, the task runs the next time you open Neologos.
